Hi, I’m **Abhimanyu**. I’m a 28 year old man living in Bangalore, India. I develop software for a living (see [Work](/work) for more). Thanks for stopping by.

This website serves two people - me, the author and you, the reader. As for me, the primary reason for putting time and effort into making this website is to start a virtuous cycle. In short, my hope is that as I write here, I’ll be driven to read more which will lead to me becoming a better writer while will lead to me writing more.

Also, this website is a good excuse to really look into my soul so that I don’t lie to you. For example, the “Currently Interested in” list below started out pretty big, but as I introspected more and paid attention to what I actually do rather than what I say the list kept shrinking.

As for you, the reader, I promise to respect your attention through good typography, useful content and no AI generated writing. I would not treat this website as my personal notepad or a place for half-baked thoughts. I promise to spend atleast 40 hours on a piece of writing before hitting the publish button.

There will be posts with my subjective opinions and thoughts, the reader can be assured that those opinion and thoughts have been thought through for hours before being put to paper. AI might be used to research, fact checking and spellchecks but not a single word on this website will be written by AI. Even if I ask AI to rephrase something, I’ll make sure to type it out using my human hands.
