I’ll never forget the day it arrived. It was 28th of Jan, 2026. Delivery guy called and I ran to the gates, stumbling over my feet. I uttered those 4 letters - the OTP and there it was in my hands. I ran back to my apartment making sure not to stumble this time otherwise I could’ve hurt my precious. I calmed myself down, sat on my chair and started ~~undressing~~ unwrapping it. Got distracted by those damn bubble wrappers for a while - why are they so fun?!! Did you know that the inventors of bubble wrappers were trying to make textured wallpapers, but they failed and pivoted to bubble wrappers when IBM started using that to ship computers. One of humanity’s greatest invention was a pivot!

Anyways, once I had popped all those motherfuckers, I finally saw eye to eye with the new potential love of my life - “Keychron K8 Wireless Mechanical Keyboard, RGB backlight aluminum version with Gateron mechanical brown switches”. What a cute name. I showed it around excitedly to my friends but for some reason they gave me side eye looks that said “Calm down bro, it’s just a bunch of keys on a rectangular board”. I told “Keychron K8 Wireless Mechanical Keyboard, RGB backlight aluminum version with Gateron mechanical brown switches” to not be sad and that “everyone will like you once they get to know you”. No one has felt what smashing on your long space bar feels like.

As I’m writing this, we’ve been together for a month. The relationship wasn’t smooth sailing at first. My writing speed dropped to abysmal 60 wpm as I was getting used to the curves and grooves of “Keychron K8 Wireless Mechanical Keyboard, RGB backlight aluminum version with Gateron mechanical brown switches”. But I persisted. I knew that spending good amount of time with each other was the only way we could make this work. We used to go to this place called play.typeracer.com on dates. After a few weeks, we realized that we really were a good match as our typing speed soared to 100s. We were like those famous duos - Batman and robin, Sherlock and Watson, [Mozart and Mozart’s friend.](https://youtu.be/1i2X9dNAwCc?si=3RMnKwXL-rKH5_g1)

## What love does to you

There’s this funny and simple thing that the brain does. Whenever it feels pain or anticipates pain while doing a certain activity, it starts flinching away from it subconsciously. Every time you’ll think of doing that activity, hormones will be released which will make you feel dread. Your body will resist doing it and you’ll have to use your finite source of willpower to actually do it.

Personally, sitting with my laptop had become that activity for me. I had subconsciously started flinching away from it without even knowing. I was only doing it because it was important for the job and used to feel drained after.

Now in retrospect, I can see it. I used to sit hunch backed over a 13 inch MacBook air using the keyboard with that shallow key travel. At the end of the day, my back and knuckles used to hurt, but I brushed them off to tiredness and the usual “eh, whatever”.

I used to watch my senior colleague at work still going strong merrily clacking keys on his keyboard late into the evenings while being a decade older than me. He is usually a fountain of energy but there was something more to it. He clacked on a mechanical keyboard and used a large monitor while sitting in a sort of alert laid back posture. I noticed and learnt.

Now, that I have almost the same sort of setup, the effects on my brain have been profound. Imagine your body not flinching away from the activity that your self worth depends on. Imagine wanting to spend more time on the activity that you want to get good at.

The long term effects of this will be incredible. This infusion of beauty in one part of my life has kickstarted something inside of me where I’m now trying to remove any source of pains or discomfort which make me want to spend less time doing a thing that I want to do. I’ve changed the fonts in my terminal emulator (ghostty) to Monaco and the theme to Medallion. I’ve learnt kon-mari folding so that my wardrobe can be distinguished from a pile of garbage. I’ve started taking baths every day. Life is beautiful.
